## Summary
All habit data lives on the device; the app works with no network.

## Why it matters
Users track private habits. No account and no server means nothing to leak and
nothing to run — see [[2026-06-10-no-account-no-server]].

## Implications
- History is the only record of a user's streaks; losing it loses their progress.
- Any feature that sends data off the device needs a new decision.
