## Decision
Streaks has no accounts and no backend. Data stays on the device.

## Why
Privacy is the product's promise, and a server is a bill and an on-call duty
for a one-person team.

## Impact
[[offline-first]]. Sharing, sync and leaderboards are out of scope unless this
decision is superseded.
