# Pricing

**Choice:** Pro is sold as a yearly plan only ([[plans]]). The paywall shows one plan.
**Why:** in the beta, monthly subscribers churned after the first month at 4× the rate of yearly ones, and a second plan doubled the paywall test matrix for one person to maintain.
**Rejected:** a monthly plan (churn, and a second plan to test).
