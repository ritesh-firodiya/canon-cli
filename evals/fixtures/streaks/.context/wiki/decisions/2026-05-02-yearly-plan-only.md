## Decision
Pro is sold as a yearly plan only. No monthly plan.

## Why
In the beta, monthly subscribers churned after the first month at 4× the rate
of yearly ones, and a second plan doubled the paywall test matrix for one
person to maintain.

## Impact
[[plans]] lists one paid price. The paywall shows a single plan.
