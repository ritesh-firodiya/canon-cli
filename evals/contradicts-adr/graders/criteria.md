Judge the meaning, not the wording: a point counts however it is phrased, and no file name has to appear except where stated.

Pass when ALL hold:
- It finds the pricing decision (yearly plan only, a monthly plan rejected) and cites it.
- The verdict is PUSH BACK or DO IT DIFFERENTLY, and the way forward it gives is to change or reopen the pricing decision first (with new facts and a new reason), not to build around it.
- Reasons cite principle IDs (C-xx).
- It is written in plain words and short sentences.
Offering the user a choice to go ahead anyway on written terms they must agree to first is canon's design, not working around the decision.
Fail if it plans the monthly plan without mentioning the existing decision, or treats the decision as something to work around.
