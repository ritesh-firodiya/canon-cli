What each click does

Terminals have no hover, so every short item explains itself on click: a one-line panel under the band. A second click closes it. Actions (☀, size, cache) act and say what they did. No click approves anything.

ItemClick
SunA sun. White outlined ☼ is the default: the Mac sleeps as usual. Green filled ☀ keeps the Mac from sleeping while this session is open (caffeinate). It stops by itself when the session ends.
PhasePanel: every phase and where the task is.
WAITING ON YOUPanel: what to review and the approve command.
TaskOpens the task's page in your editor. Shown in words, never by ID; cut at 40 characters. The band cannot change the task.
Reply sizeOne letter: S, M or L. A click moves to the next size and says what it means. Holds for the session, starts at M, keeps the cache.
Model·effortShort form: son·med, opus·hi. Grey when canon picked, amber when you chose, ? when it could not decide and used your settings. Panel: why it was picked, and choose yourself. Only with features: [routing].
Warnings, errorsPanel: the list.
CacheSends one tiny message so the hour starts again. Its cost is in the label. Only when warm and no reply is running.

Reply sizes

SizeWhat Claude writes
SThe answer in one to three lines. No headings, no tables.
MThe answer first, then the key points as a short bullet list.
LThe answer first, then full detail: steps, reasons, trade-offs and what was not checked.
